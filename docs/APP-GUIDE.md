# Limen iPhone 1.2 working-tree guide

This guide describes the current implementation. Check the audit's final verification/release report before treating it as a shipped, fully validated build.

Start with your name and choose tenant, private landlord or agency. A one-part or single-letter name is accepted. Contact details are optional and reusable; a supplied phone number must be valid for its selected country. An agency name is kept separate from the contractual landlord, attending representative and authority/mandate.

Enter the property, date/time, canton and actual contractual participants. Add every co-tenant or landlord who needs an independent outcome or copy. Address suggestions are optional: activating them sends entered address text to swisstopo. Manual entry works offline. The object's canton and legal locality/category are separate from any participant's contact address and the rooms selected for inspection.

Choose and walk the rooms in a useful order. Name any room, add observations and photographs, inspect supplied objects, and move to the next room. A claim, repair/cost note and each tenant's response are distinct statements. Recording normal wear does not remove another participant's claim. Open a photograph to inspect/zoom it. If a room or component was inaccessible or unavailable, record the reason rather than claiming it was checked.

Camera access is requested when you choose the camera. If you previously denied it, use the offered Settings action or choose an existing picture from Photos. Device restrictions and failed photo processing show a persistent explanation; cancelling the camera does not create a failure. Photo intake accepts files up to 32 MiB and 80 megapixels.

Record keys/cards/fobs/remotes with identifiers, expected/transferred counts and discrepancy reasons. Do not enter working access codes in shared identifiers. Repeatable meter readings can include unit, value, date/time, note and photograph. Legacy saved key and meter fields remain readable. If Keys is omitted, explain why; deleting its card is not an answer to the transfer question. Record actual possession/key transfer separately from the appointment date.

The dossier can contain contract information, explicit no deposit or unknown deposit, notes and supporting documents. Attach a PDF/image lease, rent form, mandate, prior report, deposit or delivery proof, or enter an external reference. Files are validated by content and size. Missing paperwork may be described in a scope or legal-check note without pretending it was available. A deposited amount, receipt and insurance product are different facts; the app does not certify them.

Review each applicable legal check. Give each unresolved, disputed or inapplicable check its own explanation. Initial-rent guidance uses the lease conclusion year and, where required, NE municipality/dwelling category or VD district. Official links and the competent-authority directory help verify the situation. A planning date appears only when actual transfer, an agreed number of days and a contractual clause are recorded. It is not a safe statutory waiting period or proof that a legal notice was served.

Read the complete document preview, including photos, scope, claims, individual positions/reservations, attachments and legal information. Switch between the PDF and its structured accessible view before acknowledging review. Document labels follow the chosen document language; entered testimony stays in its original language. Record the participants' understood language and make the controller/privacy notice available. Each participant has their own drawn signature or an honest refused/absent/unable-to-draw outcome. These alternatives are not equivalent to drawn ink or a qualified electronic signature. A material edit requires renewed review and warns which existing signers are affected.

At larger text sizes, scroll through the full signer name and signature purpose to the drawing surface. Clear, Cancel and Save remain available below the content.

In a multi-line note, Return adds a line; the Done shown above the keyboard ends editing, and dragging the list also closes the keyboard.

An invalid date/time or number remains a draft value and blocks finalization until corrected; use the explicit restore action to recover a last valid field value. You can save and resume an incomplete draft. If saving fails, keep the record open and retry; a failed close does not imply a safe saved copy. Unreadable library entries are shown for recovery, with the damaged original preserved when restoring a last-good copy.

Finalization freezes content and legal results. Later corrections belong in an amendment. An exit seeded from a final entry preserves contractual parties and offers the entry evidence read-only; it does not copy old damage as a new allegation. Check representation authority again for the new inspection.

Export the PDF and HTML companion, then Open/Share the retained copy. The library retains exact private copies and export history, including document language and delivery revision. Record actual delivery separately for each participant; re-exporting records the newly rendered delivery state while preserving earlier exact copies. Restored foreign paths/URIs are metadata, so retrieval resolves retained files on this device. A historic PDF that was never retained cannot be promised byte-identical reconstruction.

Use library search and draft/final filters to find records and entry/exit/amendment relationships. The optional device-credential gate protects library/settings access; it does not encrypt individual files or hide the active handover from participants reviewing it.

Back up selected records with their referenced evidence, supporting files and retained exports. A password encrypts the archive. An explicitly selected export without a password is unencrypted and readable by anyone who receives it. Public exports are outside private storage in Files, in the Limen app folder. Preserve passwords and copies responsibly; send rights/retention requests to the person or organisation operating this installation. Original private evidence and recovery material may remain until the record is deleted, so removing a photo from a draft is not a secure-erasure claim.

For technical compatibility, legal coverage and remaining validation, see [the implementation ledger](audits/2026-10-13/IMPLEMENTATION.md) and [legal mapping](audits/2026-10-13/LEGAL-MAPPING.md).
