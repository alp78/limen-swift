# Limen iPhone 1.2.7 working-tree guide

This guide describes the current implementation. Check the audit's final verification/release report and the 1.2.7 handoff before treating it as a shipped, fully validated build.

Start with your name and choose tenant, private landlord or agency. A one-part or single-letter name is accepted. Contact details are optional and reusable; a supplied phone number must be valid for its selected country. An agency name is kept separate from the contractual landlord, attending representative and authority/mandate. The form ends in two keys that wait for the name: Brouillon saves the record and returns to the list, Valider opens it.

The record's home is the hub: INSPECTION, DOSSIER and CONSTAT, the last opening the review, the signatures and the export, with the signature state as its readout. Under the doors, what is still missing is listed one line at a time; Tout afficher shows the rest, and each line opens the place where the data goes. A line names what it rejects: the rooms, items or parties concerned, or each value the dossier check refused, so a rent typed as text or a wear estimate without its cost is named rather than hidden behind three sections that are fine. The dossier's section chips tick by the same checks as the finalization gate, so a ticked chip never sits beside a blocked FINALISER.

Enter the property, date/time, canton and actual contractual participants. Add every co-tenant or landlord who needs an independent outcome or copy. Official address suggestions appear by themselves once three characters are typed in a Swiss address field, which sends the typed text to swisstopo whenever the phone is online; the fields stay free to type offline or when nothing matches, and a service that cannot be reached is shown as a status line. The object's canton and legal locality/category are separate from any participant's contact address and the rooms selected for inspection.

Choose and walk the rooms in a useful order. Name any room, add observations and photographs, inspect supplied objects, and move to the next room. On a room, the actions sit in one row: the room's actions behind one key, which opens into up, down, clear findings and remove room, and Pièce suivante at the end. A claim, repair/cost note and each tenant's response are distinct statements. Recording normal wear does not remove another participant's claim. Open a photograph to inspect/zoom it. If a room or component was inaccessible or unavailable, record the reason in the Motif field rather than claiming it was checked; a reason prints only while the thing it excuses is still missing, and the Motif fields are offered only for the components that are on the record, plus Keys while its card is off it. Removing a meter reading, a key or access device, a supporting document, a follow-up, a wear estimate, a party or a kind of key offers it back for a few seconds, as removing a finding or a photograph does, and the undo bar is drawn over the finding editor as well as the flow.

Camera access is requested when you choose the camera. If you previously denied it, use the offered Settings action or choose an existing picture from Photos. Device restrictions and failed photo processing show a persistent explanation; cancelling the camera does not create a failure. A picture chosen from Photos is copied by the record itself, so leaving the finding editor while it is still arriving does not lose it, and a photograph or document that could not be read or stored says so in a whole sentence. A signature waits for a photograph or document still being stored: the pad keeps its ink and says so. Photographs, supporting documents and backups have no size limit: a photograph is accepted when it decodes, a document when it opens, and a backup of any size is sealed and opened a buffer at a time, so one larger than the phone's memory saves and restores as long as the phone has room for it.

Record keys/cards/fobs/remotes with identifiers, expected/transferred counts and discrepancy reasons. Do not enter working access codes in shared identifiers. Repeatable meter readings can include unit, value, date/time, note and photograph. Legacy saved key and meter fields remain readable. If Keys is omitted, explain why; deleting its card is not an answer to the transfer question. Record actual possession/key transfer separately from the appointment date. An exit seeded from an entry starts with the entry's kinds of key and empty counts, without the copies declaration and without the entry's notification clause, which only an entry edits.

The dossier can contain contract information, explicit no deposit or unknown deposit, notes and supporting documents. Attach a PDF/image lease, rent form, mandate, prior report, deposit or delivery proof, or enter an external reference. Files are validated by content and size. Missing paperwork may be described in a scope or legal-check note without pretending it was available. A deposited amount, receipt and insurance product are different facts; the app does not certify them.

Review each applicable legal check. Give each unresolved, disputed or inapplicable check its own explanation. Initial-rent guidance uses the lease conclusion year and, where required, NE municipality/dwelling category or VD district. Official links and the competent-authority directory help verify the situation. A planning date appears only when actual transfer, an agreed number of days and a contractual clause are recorded. It is not a safe statutory waiting period or proof that a legal notice was served.

Read the complete document preview, including photos, scope, claims, individual positions/reservations, attachments and legal information; the review step lists the same supplementary sections the paper prints, read from the same builder. The printed record shows only what the screen still offers: the municipality and the district for their cantons, a representative's authority while a representative is named, a removed tenant's position never, the deposit's fields for the chosen kind alone, and the wear basis and the repair deadline in the screen's words. A country in a foreign contact address is named in the document's language. The document is drawn in the app's own style: the Day palette, panels with rounded corners and a navy border, pills and chips, the system typeface in three weights and no monospace. Page 1 is a cover with the property, the parties, the record's status with its reference and fingerprint, and a contents list whose page numbers the export checks against the pages it printed; a document whose contents would name a wrong page is refused rather than exported. The body is one panel per section: the canton checks, the data controller, the contract, the deposit, the meters and the keys when the record carries them, then one panel per room, amber when it holds findings, with the finding number in a navy badge, its words, the landlord's claim as an active chip, the repair party, the deadline, the photograph count and the page of its photographs in the annex; then the objects, the remarks, the signatures, the copies delivered and the deposit release. A panel too tall for the space left continues on the next page under its title with a Continued pill, and the footer carries the page number out of the total on every sheet. The photo annex is organised the way the handover was walked: a contents page, then one section per room opening on its own page under the room's name, then the supplied objects, the meters and anything left. Each photograph sits large in a panel, clipped to rounded corners under a navy border, with its legend beside or under it: the room, the finding's number and words as the body prints them, who is held to it, and when it was taken. The photographs go into the PDF as JPEG at the resolution their printed size needs, so a record of thirty photographs makes a PDF of a few megabytes; the HTML copy embeds the same images. Switch between the PDF and its HTML copy, then Valider under the document to acknowledge the review; Done in the bar only closes it. Document labels follow the chosen document language; entered testimony stays in its original language. Record the participants' understood language. The privacy information and the controller details are under Data, backup and privacy, not on the handover form. Each participant has their own drawn signature or an honest refused/absent/cannot-sign outcome. These alternatives are not equivalent to drawn ink or a qualified electronic signature. A material edit requires renewed review and warns which existing signers are affected.

At larger text sizes, scroll through the full signer name and signature purpose to the drawing surface. Clear, Cancel and Save remain available below the content.

In a multi-line note, Return adds a line; the Done shown above the keyboard ends editing, and dragging the list also closes the keyboard.

An invalid date/time or number remains a draft value and blocks finalization until corrected; the undo key beside the field restores its last valid value. You can save and resume an incomplete draft. If saving fails, keep the record open and retry; a failed close does not imply a safe saved copy. Unreadable library entries are shown for recovery, with the damaged original preserved when restoring a last-good copy; a recovery that fails says so under its key. Typing in the street or the place no longer clears the cantonal answers; a change of canton does.

Finalization freezes content and legal results. Later corrections belong in an amendment. An exit seeded from a final entry preserves contractual parties and offers the entry evidence read-only; it does not copy old damage as a new allegation. Check representation authority again for the new inspection. An entry that a finalized amendment replaced no longer offers the exit: the card says to start it from the amendment. An amendment copies only the photographs and documents its draft refers to, and a copy that fails leaves nothing behind.

Export the PDF and HTML companion, then Open/Share the retained copy. The files are named by the address and the handover's day, such as Avenue-de-la-Mesure-30-2026-10-21.pdf; a second export of the same record on the same day is numbered. The library retains exact private copies and export history; each line of the history says its language and time, and whether it predates the latest delivery change. The result card says whether the copy shown was just written to Files or is the app's own retained copy. A backup of the open record alone is offered from the export step. Record actual delivery separately for each participant; re-exporting records the newly rendered delivery state while preserving earlier exact copies. Restored foreign paths/URIs are metadata, so retrieval resolves retained files on this device. A historic PDF that was never retained cannot be promised byte-identical reconstruction.

Use library search and draft/final filters to find records and entry/exit/amendment relationships. A draft entry's card offers FINALISER, which opens it at the signature step; a finalized entry's card offers SORTIE, which seeds the exit from it. The optional device-credential gate protects library/settings access; it does not encrypt individual files or hide the active handover from participants reviewing it, and it stands aside on a phone whose passcode was removed afterwards.

Back up selected records with their referenced evidence, supporting files and retained exports. JPEG photographs are repacked losslessly as JPEG XL inside the backup, about a fifth smaller, and rebuilt byte for byte on restore, so every recorded evidence hash holds; a backup with repacked photographs is archive version 2, which Limen 1.2.5 or later on either platform restores, and one without stays version 1. A password encrypts the archive. An explicitly selected export without a password is unencrypted and readable by anyone who receives it. A backup is refused, with the reason and the record named, when a record cannot be read or its evidence no longer matches what the record recorded, because a restore would reject it; the other records can be backed up one at a time from their export step. A restore unpacks the evidence once and moves it into place, so it needs free space for about the size of the backup, about twice that for a password-protected one, which is decrypted to a copy first; a wrong password is refused after the first chunk; a stage left by a restore or an amendment that was killed part way is swept when the app next starts; and evidence folders that no record owns are moved into recovery rather than blocking their record. Public exports are outside private storage in Files, in the Limen app folder. Preserve passwords and copies responsibly; send rights/retention requests to the person or organisation operating this installation. Original private evidence and recovery material may remain until the record is deleted, so removing a photo from a draft is not a secure-erasure claim.
