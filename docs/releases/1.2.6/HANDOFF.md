# Limen iPhone 1.2.6 (8), the record in the app's own style, 21 October 2026

Written 21 October 2026 against Swift commit `0540e3e` (1.2.5 (7), the annex by room, the unbounded backups and JPEG XL) and Android `origin/main` at `8254bdc`, 1.2.6 (versionCode 11), committed the same afternoon on top of `94c7cf7`. The request is `_scratch/ios-record-redesign-1.2.6.md`. It assumed `_scratch/ios-annex-backups-1.2.5.md` had been carried out, and it had: the iPhone already printed Android 1.2.4's annex by room with 200 dpi JPEG plates, had no size limits and repacked backups as JPEG XL, so the annex composition was kept and only restyled. The Android checkout at `/Users/aperi/DEV/ANDROID/limen` was already at `8254bdc` and clean; nothing in the Android repository or on the website was modified.

Three things the owner asked to be told before anything else:

- **The iPhone's theme differed from the Android palette in two values, and the request said to stop and report.** The cause is in the history, not in a decision: Android's audit commit `eb5c038` changed the Day palette's `warningOutline` from `#B26A00` to `#835000` and its `disabledInk` from `#586272` to `#A7ADB8`, and the iPhone's parity pass (`ca3e6bc`) carried the audit's screens but left these two numbers at their pre-audit values. Android is the specification, and Android 1.2.6 reads both the screen and the paper from one `LimenPalette`, so this port does the same: `LimenPalette` in LimenKit holds Android's thirteen values, the SwiftUI Day theme reads it, and the printed record reads it. The screen changed with it: the warning outline on the iPhone is now the darker amber `#835000` and the disabled ink the lighter `#A7ADB8`, as on the Pixel. If the owner wants the old screen values back, they are two constants in `LimenPalette`, but then the paper and the screen would differ from Android's. `PdfTokensTests.everyColourIsTheScreensColour` asserts the two.
- **The strings generator could not read the new `defectCount`.** Android 1.2.6 added it as a `when (lang)` whose branches are `if (n == 1) "1 defect" else "$n defects"`, a shape `tools/strings.py` did not know (it knew `if (param == Enum.CASE)`). The generator now takes a number after `==` as well, with a unit test for the shape, and `Str.swift` was regenerated: 385 declarations, the four new strings among them. `make check-generated` is clean.
- **The six-room record prints on 44 pages here and 42 on the Pixel, and the two-page German record prints on two.** The body of the six-room record breaks two pages later because the professional record this port's export tests build carries more than Android's `sample()` (a controller, a contract, a deposit, meters, two receipts, remarks) and San Francisco measures differently from Roboto; the annex is 36 cards on 36 pages plus six section pages on both phones. The numbers and what was compared are in the verification section.

## What changed

**The generator is one file per piece**, mirroring Android's `data/pdf/` under `LimenKit/Sources/Export/`:

| Android | iPhone | What it holds |
|---|---|---|
| `PdfTokens.kt` | `PdfTokens.swift` | Every size from the app's point and type tokens at 1.6 units per point on the 1240 by 1754 grid, the radii, the paddings, the colours from `LimenPalette`, the face name. No graphics type in it |
| `theme/LimenPalette.kt` | `LimenKit/Sources/Theme/LimenPalette.swift` | The Day palette as plain RGB values, read by the SwiftUI theme and the paper |
| `Paginator.kt` | `PdfPaginator.swift` | Row heights in, closed segments out: greedy fill, kept rows leaving with their successor, a first segment of fewer than two rows moving the panel to a fresh page, a row taller than a page placed alone |
| `Paints.kt` | `PdfPaints.swift` | `Face` (font, colour, tracking as an em fraction, the baseline that centres it in a line) and `Paints`, three weights of the system face and the flat fills and strokes |
| `Blocks.kt` | `PdfBlocks.swift` | `Row`, the `Block` protocol, `TextMeasure`, `Text`, `Line`, `Gap`, `Fields`, `Chip` and `Chips`, `Finding`, `ItemGrid`, `Ink`, `Contents`; each block lays itself out as atomic rows at a width and writes its own HTML once |
| `Sheet.kt` | `PdfSheet.swift` | `Tone`, `PillTone`, `Pill`, `Panel`, `Mark`, and the `Sheet`: pages, the running header, the "Page n / N" footer, `ensure`, `mark`, `pill`, `heading`, `panel` (segments, the title repeated with a Continued pill), `frame`, `block`, and the drawing primitives, which are flat fills and strokes of rounded rectangles, lines, text and a bitmap under a rounded clip |
| `Outline.kt` | `PdfOutline.swift` | The sections a record prints, in the paper canon's order, with the predicates the drawing uses |
| `Cover.kt` | `PdfCover.swift` | Page 1: the banner, the seal, the kind pill, the address block, the object, the parties, the status and the contents panels |
| `Dossier.kt` | `PdfDossier.swift` | The canton checks, the data controller, the contract, the deposit, the meters, the keys |
| `Rooms.kt` | `PdfRooms.swift` | One panel per room, amber with its findings inside, the annex pointer chips first |
| `Findings.kt` | `PdfFindings.swift` | The finding block and the stray findings panel |
| `Objects.kt` | `PdfObjects.swift` | The toggle inventory and one panel per object group |
| `Closing.kt` | `PdfClosing.swift` | The supplemental panels, the remarks, the signatures and the historical cost signature, the copies delivered, the deposit release |
| `Annex.kt` | `PdfAnnex.swift` | The 1.2.5 composition in the panel look: the heading with its photo pill, the contents block, one heading per section, cards as raised panels with the plate clipped to the thumbnail corners under a navy border |
| `PdfExport.kt` | `PdfExport.swift` | The orchestrator: the plan, the counting pass, the check of the marks against the plan, the drawing pass, the check of its marks against the first, the seal decoded once at 256 pixels |
| `HtmlCompanion.kt` | `HtmlDocument.swift` | `HtmlSink` with the banner, headings with anchors, panels with pills, fields, chips, findings, the contents list, the metadata list and the figures, in Android's stylesheet |

The drawing code the pieces replace is gone: the old `PdfSheet` with its typewriter faces, the section functions in `PdfExport`, the old annex. `PdfExport.Sheet` keeps the page bookkeeping the new sheet reuses (the flipped and scaled page, the JPEG data provider for a plate, the memory output for the counting pass) and nothing else.

**The cover** is the landing's navy field with the seal at 128 units, "LIMEN" in the display face, the protocol kind beneath it in the on-navy dim, the handover kind as a paper pill, and the address and place with the moment and the canton right-aligned; then four panels the contents does not list: the rental object, the parties with who compiled the record, the record's status with the Finalized or Draft pill, the reference and the fingerprint, and the contents with each section's page. A contents that runs onto a second page is followed by the body on that page, which the German sample shows.

**The body** is one panel per section with the title in small capitals and a pill at the right, 25.6 units of padding, a navy or amber border inset by half its width. A panel that does not fit the space left is drawn in closed segments, each repeating the title with a Continued pill, by the paginator the eleven Android cases describe; a heading needs 260 units left. Each room is one panel, amber when it has findings, its pill the defect count, "Room inspected" or "Not inspected", its first row the photograph count and the annex page as chips; a finding is a navy badge with its number beside the where line in capitals, its words, chips for the landlord's claim (active), the repair party, the deadline, the photograph count and the tenant's response, then its notes and the participants' positions, every row kept with the next so a finding moves to a page whole. The keys print as chips, the objects as a name with chips and their findings under them, the receipts as a name with the method and the moment as chips. Independent facts no longer share a line joined with a middle dot: `RecordContent` prints one fact per line, a colon between a label and its value, a role in parentheses after a name, as the Android diff of `RecordContent.kt` does.

**The two passes and the check.** `Outline.plan` lists the sections before anything is drawn. The counting pass draws with a total of 0 and no HTML, decodes no photograph, and records a mark for each section as it opens; the export checks the marks against the plan, hands the pages to the drawing pass for the contents and the annex pointers, and after the drawing pass checks its marks against the counted ones, throwing `ExportError.contentsMismatch` rather than printing a wrong page under a signed document. The preview goes through the same two passes and the same check: it is a PDF with a "Page n / N" footer here, where Android's preview is a single-pass HTML.

**The HTML copy** is written by each block once, before its panel is laid out, with `<header class="banner">`, `<h2 id="cN">` headings, `<section class="panel raised|warning">` panels with `<span class="pill tone">`, `<dl class="grid">` fields, `<ul class="chips">`, `<article class="finding">` with the badge, `<nav class="contents">` with anchors, and the annex legend's metadata as `<ul class="meta">`; the anchors are `c0`, `c1`, … in mark order. The stylesheet is Android's `STYLE` in the same hex values, with the iPhone's face first in the stack and without the `max-width` on `main`, under the preview's Content Security Policy, which allows exactly one inline sheet.

**The annex** keeps the 1.2.5 composition (the sections and cards of `AnnexContent`, the size rule, the legend's two columns, the centred continuation pages, the run-on card) in the panel look: a heading "Photo annex" with a Dim photo-count pill over the navy rule, a contents block outside any panel when there is more than one section, each section's heading at level 1 keyed by the section's key so the room panels can cite its page, and each card a raised panel with the photograph clipped to the thumbnail radius under a navy border. The clip is a path in the content stream: `pdfimages -list` shows the 36 plates of the six-room record as `jpeg` with no soft mask, and `ExportTests` still finds every plate as one DCT stream whose bytes are the HTML copy's. `AnnexContent.Section` gained its `key` (the room's id, or `objects`, `defects`, `meters`, `photos`).

**The seal** is decoded once per pass at 256 pixels and drawn on every page from the same `CGImage`, which Core Graphics embeds once (object 6 in every image table, 98 KB), where 512 pixels had been 374 KB of a 500 KB record on Android.

**Deviations recorded.** The draft preview names each participant whose outcome is still missing inside the signature panel ("Name (Role)", "Not signed", the reservation), the review this port added in the audit; a finalized record has every required outcome and prints as Android's does. The understood-language section prints only when a language was recorded, as Android's `extraContent` does, with the translation notice under it (the old port printed the notice alone, which put a section on every record Android does not plan). The deposit release prints on an exit with an unknown deposit as "To verify", as Android does since 1.2.6; the old port left it out. The document's name stays the short `<address>-<handover day>.pdf` of 1.2.5. The costs plane lists its current signatures only, as Android does; the old port listed every costs signature.

**Version** is 1.2.6 build 8 in `Project.swift`, asserted by `BundleTests`; `make device` put it on the iPhone. Build 7 (1.2.5) was never uploaded, so build 8's TestFlight note in [RELEASE-NOTES.md](RELEASE-NOTES.md) names the 1.2.5 changes as well as the redesign. The notes are in the five languages, in the iPhone's own words, and name no other product and no tester.
