# Release notes for Limen Traditionis 1.2.6 (8)

Prepared 21 October 2026 and uploaded the same evening with build 8 (see the last section). One note per language. Build 7 (1.2.5) was built in Release on the iPhone and never uploaded, so build 8 is the first upload after 1.2.2 (6) and its note names the three things that changed since: the annex by room and the unbounded, repacked backups of 1.2.5, and the document in the app's own style of 1.2.6. The sentences on the document are the iPhone's own words for what Android's `docs/listing/release-notes.md` says under versionCode 11; they name no other product and no tester. Each block is well under App Store Connect's 4,000-character limit for "What's New" and TestFlight's "What to Test". App Store Connect has no Romansh locale; the Romansh text is kept for the in-app and TestFlight notes should a place for it appear.

Native Romansh review remains pending, as it does for the Android listing.

## en-US

```
The exported record now looks like the app. It opens with a cover: the property, the parties, the record's status with its reference, and a contents list whose page numbers the app checks against the pages it printed. Then one panel per room with its numbered defects, the keys and meter readings as the app shows them, the signatures, and the photo annex in the same panels. Same colours, rounded corners and typeface as on screen, with the page number on every sheet. The photo annex is organised by room, each photograph large in a frame with its room, the defect it documents, who is held responsible and when it was taken; a handover with 30 photographs makes a PDF of about 8 MB. Backups, photographs and documents have no size limit, and backups are about a fifth smaller, their photographs repacked losslessly and restored byte for byte. A backup made with this version restores in Limen 1.2.5 or later; older backups restore as before.
```

## Where they went

The App Store Connect locales of the app are `en-US`, `fr-FR`, `de-DE` and `it` (not `it-IT`, see the vault's environment note). Build 6 (1.2.2) was uploaded on 15 October 2026, submitted to Beta App Review on 16 October and approved; build 7 was never uploaded. Build 8 was uploaded on 21 October 2026 (delivery `9fa56942-a00b-411d-a728-e8d4531a4915`) and the four "What to Test" notes below were written to its `betaBuildLocalizations` and read back identical. The "What's New" notes above were not: the store version, renamed from 1.1.2 to 1.2.6 with build 8 attached, is the app's first, and Apple refuses "What's New" on a first version (`409 STATE_ERROR`, "Attribute 'whatsNew' cannot be edited at this time"). They are kept here for the first update after the app goes on sale. The record of the upload is in [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md).

## TestFlight "What to Test" for build 8

The build-6 note's opening and its two asks stay; the middle paragraph names what changed since build 6, because the document, the annex and the backup are the three things this build touches and the three things most worth a second phone.

### en-US

```
Second external build. Put it through a real handover if you can, a realistic one if not.

Start a record, pick your side and press Validate. Fill the dossier, add findings with photographs to several rooms, then open RECORD: review, have both parties sign, finalize and export.

What changed: the exported PDF is drawn in the app's own style, with a cover, a contents list, one panel per room with its numbered defects, and the photo annex organised by room with each photograph large in a frame beside its context. Please read the whole document once, check that every page the contents names is the right one, and tell us whether the printed record reads as the record you made. Backups have no size limit any more and come out about a fifth smaller; please make one with a password and restore it on a second phone, and check that every photograph is back.

Please report anything that reads wrong in your language, any cantonal rule that does not match your practice, and anything the app lets you do that a handover should not allow.
```
