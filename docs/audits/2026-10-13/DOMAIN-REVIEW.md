## Confirmed source behaviour

- Stable participant IDs select independent signatures, reservations and delivery receipts. Legacy role fallback does not inject identifiers into frozen old records. Explicit empty reservations survive JSON round trips and supersede prior ink text.
- `RecordContent.extraContent` supplies the structured additions to both PDF and HTML. It includes pending legacy role reservations through `reservationFor`, independent tenant positions, scope notes, locality facts, evidence provenance, supporting-document manifests and estimate inputs. This selects facts; it does not prove those facts or deliver notice to another party.
- Review acknowledgement is bound to the exact record ID and content revision of the generated preview. A material edit invalidates it. Preview generation clears the previous preview before suspension and checks the origin after completion.
- Frozen legal snapshots take precedence. Old finalized records without a snapshot use the explicit legacy resolver and never acquire a newly dated source list merely by previewing or re-exporting. Exact retained outputs remain the preferred historical document.
- Finalized Store updates preserve the canonical evidence content and fingerprint while allowing permitted delivery/export metadata changes. Export rendering checks decoded evidence separately from hashes. The HTML generated by the shared rendering pass escapes entered text and has a restrictive content-security policy; its in-app viewer disables JavaScript.
- ZIP reading checks size ceilings, central/local consistency, descriptors, overlap, entry paths and symbolic-link metadata before installation. Archive installation copies only referenced evidence and preserves originals in quarantine for explicit corrupt-record repair.
- Final source triage found missing camera denial/recovery and silent processing failures. The camera now requests authorization, offers Settings after denial, explains device restrictions, and distinguishes cancellation from failure. Its coordinator owns a cancellable encoding operation and unique private slot; pixel bounds precede direct-to-file encoding, byte limits and decoding validation follow it, and cancelled results delete only their own file. Metadata crosses the concurrency boundary as property-list bytes. Physical permission/cancellation/low-storage verification remains part of the final device report.
- Executed simulator tests denied the actual camera prompt and cancelled actual Files/authentication prompts, preserving the draft or disabled gate through relaunch. This simulator exposed camera and authentication capabilities; those passes do not certify an unavailable-hardware branch. Deterministic app tests also rejected late photo results after a record/amendment switch, owner deletion or finalization, preserved unrelated captures, and verified successful exact byte/hash retention.

## Platform and schema limits

The shared `ExportCopy` schema carries one PDF SHA-256, not a separate HTML digest. Do not claim that a restored companion's bytes are authenticated by that PDF digest. An encrypted archive authenticates its package in transit/storage; it does not turn the rendered PDF into a qualified electronic signature. JavaScript being disabled does not itself attest to a companion's factual equivalence. The in-app viewer now restricts navigation; the absence of a retained HTML digest remains a schema limitation.

A structural HTML document with headings, definition lists and embedded images is an accessible alternative by design, not a completed accessibility conformance assessment. The full pre-sign path, heading navigation, descriptions, focus, magnification and understanding of non-drawn outcomes must be exercised on the actual device. The Core Graphics PDF is not described as tagged or PDF/UA conformant.

Legal coverage is a dated engineering mapping. Municipality spelling, legal dwelling categories, contract dates and individual documents remain supplied facts. Conditional/unresolved checks require specific explanations. The source register supports the implemented rules but does not constitute professional certification of every Swiss locality, document or case. Native Romansh/legal-language validation and professional tenancy review remain outstanding human gates.
